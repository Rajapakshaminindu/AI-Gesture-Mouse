## 2. In-Depth Stakeholder Interview Transcripts

### Interview Transcript #1: Healthcare & Surgical Workflow
- **Interviewee:** Dr. K. Jayasundara (Consultant Orthopedic Surgeon)
- **Setting:** District General Hospital Surgical Complex
- **Key Notes:**
  - *"In arthroscopic and orthopedic surgeries, cross-contamination is a primary post-operative infection hazard. Operating theater computers display intraoperative fluoroscopy (X-rays) and MRI 3D reconstructions."*
  - *"I cannot touch the mouse with sterile gloves on. When I need to zoom into a joint space, I have to step away or guide a nurse: 'Move up... left... zoom 20%... stop.' In almost every complex case, this causes 3–5 minutes of miscommunication and delays."*
  - *"If I could simply raise my hand in the sterile air space and pinch to zoom or swipe to navigate slices, it would make intraoperative imaging fluid and eliminate cross-contamination risk."*

### Interview Transcript #2: Accessibility & Musculoskeletal Strain
- **Interviewee:** K. Perera (Final-Year Software Engineering Undergraduate)
- **Setting:** University of Kelaniya Computer Labs
- **Key Notes:**
  - *"By third year, coding 8–10 hours daily takes a heavy physical toll. Over half my peers experience wrist stiffness, and two friends have diagnosed Carpal Tunnel Syndrome."*
  - *"Commercial ergonomic trackballs cost over 25,000 LKR ($80+ USD). That is completely unaffordable for most Sri Lankan university students."*
  - *"A lightweight system that runs on the laptop's built-in webcam, tracking my index finger and allowing me to click with a natural pinch while resting my arm on the desk chair, would eliminate physical clicking strain."*

### Interview Transcript #3: Lecture Hall & Presentation Delivery
- **Interviewee:** Senior Lecturer, Faculty of Science
- **Setting:** Hybrid Amphitheater Lecture Hall (200 Seats)
- **Key Notes:**
  - *"Standard handheld presentation clickers only have forward/backward buttons. In STEM lectures, we need to interact with live Jupyter notebooks, 3D molecular structures, and online simulation tools."*
  - *"Presenters are forced to stay glued behind the lectern to use the physical mouse, killing dynamic body language and student engagement."*
  - *"Being able to gesture naturally toward the projection screen to move the cursor, scroll documents, and click links from anywhere in the front stage would revolutionize interactive teaching."*
