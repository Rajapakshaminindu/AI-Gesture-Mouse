1 Problem, in One Line
"Physical contact computer mice cause chronic RSI in desk workers, drive cross-contamination in sterile healthcare theaters, and restrict presenters—yet existing touchless alternatives demand hundreds of dollars in proprietary hardware instead of utilizing the webcams already sitting on billions of laptops."
2 The Problem and Its Domain

For over 60 years, mechanical mice, trackpads, and touchscreens have served as the foundational interface between humans and digital systems. However, physical contact peripherals suffer from three critical, systemic failure modes:

  • Repetitive Strain Injury (RSI) & Accessibility Barriers: Over 1.3 billion individuals globally live with motor impairments (WHO). Prolonged mouse usage causes wrist pronation, tendinitis, and Carpal Tunnel Syndrome (CTS) in software engineers and students. For people with tremors or arthritis, gripping a physical mouse is excruciating or physically impossible.
  • Cross-Contamination in Sterile Healthcare: Hospital workstation mice harbor up to 1,676 microbes per square inch (3× more than a public toilet seat). In surgical theaters and cleanrooms, surgeons reviewing CT/MRI scans must either repeatedly scrub in and out or rely on verbal instructions to nurses, causing procedural delays and communication errors.
  • Spatial Tethering in Auditoriums: Lecturers and presenters are anchored behind podiums. Conventional slide remotes lack cursor navigation, drag-and-drop, and interactive simulation manipulation.

7 The Proposed Solution: GestureMouse AI

GestureMouse AI transforms any standard RGB laptop webcam into an intelligent spatial mouse controller:

  • Point & Navigate: Raising the index finger tracks coordinates smoothly across screen resolutions.
  • Pinch-to-Click: Rapid Euclidean distance calculation between index tip and thumb triggers instantaneous left-clicks.
  • Sustained Drag & Drop: Pinch-and-hold (>0.45s) locks the virtual mouse button for window dragging and text selection.
  • Kinetic Scrolling: Raising two fingers (Peace Sign) translates directly to fluid page scrolling.
  • Debounced Right-Click: 3-finger elevation triggers context menus with 0.35s debounce guard.
  • Resting Fist State: Closing fingers freezes the mouse pointer, preventing accidental triggers while resting.
8 Why This Problem Fundamentally Requires AI
  • Illumination & Skin Tone Invariance: Traditional computer vision (HSV skin thresholding) breaks under variable lighting, shadows, and diverse skin complexions. Deep neural networks (BlazePalm / Single Shot Detectors) extract semantic hand topology regardless of ambient light.
  • Monocular 3D Skeletal Regression: Flat 2D webcam pixels cannot convey spatial depth. Deep learning models infer 21 3D coordinates $(x, y, z)$ by learning biomechanical bone constraints from millions of training images.
  • Occlusion Robustness: When fingers overlap during a pinch, traditional edge-detection fails. Neural network landmark regressors maintain coordinate tracking through partial occlusion.
  • Biomechanical Tremor Suppression: Physiological micro-tremors (3–12 Hz) make pixel-level pointing impossible without AI confidence weighting paired with adaptive Exponential Moving Average (EMA) mathematical filters.
